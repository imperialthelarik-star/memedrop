Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

✅ We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans — no questions asked.

1. Subscription Refunds

If you are not satisfied with your MemeDrop subscription, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase. After 7 days, refunds are not available for the current billing period.

2. How to Request a Refund

  • Email us at memedrop.ads@gmail.com
  • Include your account email and reason for refund
  • Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days

3. Promotion Campaign Refunds

Refunds for promotion campaigns are available only if the campaign has not yet been launched. Once a campaign is live, refunds are not available.

4. Free Plan

The free plan does not involve any charges, so no refunds are applicable.

5. Exceptions

  • Accounts suspended for Terms of Service violations are not eligible for refunds
  • Partial month refunds are not available

6. Contact

For refund requests or questions, contact us at: memedrop.ads@gmail.com
