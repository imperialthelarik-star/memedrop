Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At MemeDrop, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Content you upload (memes, images)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used)
  • Payment information (processed securely by Paddle)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To process payments and subscriptions
  • To send important account notifications
  • To analyze platform usage and performance

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase and Cloudinary. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database)
  • Cloudinary (image storage)
  • Paddle (payment processing)

5. Cookies

MemeDrop uses cookies to maintain your login session and improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

7. Children's Privacy

MemeDrop is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy at any time. We will notify users of significant changes via email.

9. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at: memedrop.ads@gmail.com
